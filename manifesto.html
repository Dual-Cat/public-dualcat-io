Dual Cat’s Manifesto

Games for everyone

We offer anybody the possibility to play games for free. Our games are freemium and premium versions worth only a cup of coffee.

Games by anyone

A game is piece of art that can be created by anyone. We help talented people to live from their art by creating strong publishing partnerships with indie game studios and game developers.

Games to improve oneself

Play a game is not only about entertaining oneself, it is also a way to challenge oneself, learn about others and live unique experiences.
